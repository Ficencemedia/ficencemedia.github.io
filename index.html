About me

Nathan · Ficence Media

I train, I climb, and I design for the places that make that possible.

I grew up in a small valleys community in the Rhondda, and that shaped how I see brands: honest and down to earth, not overproduced or generic. It's why the badge I designed for Rhondda Kyokushin, a karate club back home, is built around a colliery headframe.

My day job is in the RAF. Discipline, planning and turning up when I say I will come with the territory, and I bring the same to every project. Outside of work you'll find me on a climbing wall or in the gym, which is why I design for them. I know what makes a space feel worth the membership.

I work with clients across South Wales and the rest of the UK, over video calls and messages, so where you're based doesn't slow anything down. I still take on other ambitious UK businesses too.

How I work · K.E.Y.S

8 weeks

Every brand project follows the same four stages, from our first call to final handover. I tailor the detail to your business.

  1. K

    Knowledge

    Week 1

    A 2–3 hour kick-off call to understand your gym, your members and your goals, plus a look at your competitors and gathering inspiration.

    Initial planning

  2. E

    Exploration

    Weeks 2–4

    Two or three visual directions (mood boards, colour palettes, type) for you to choose from, then the core pieces, like your logo, designed in that direction.

    Discovery and research · Design direction

  3. Y

    Yield

    Weeks 4–6

    You review the concepts and I revise until it feels right. Then I build out the full set: social templates, signage, merch, launch assets, whatever's in scope.

    Initial concepts · Final concepts

  4. S

    Succeed

    Weeks 7–8

    All your final files, guidelines on how to use them, and a short walkthrough so you're confident using the brand from day one.

    Design handover

Tell me about your gym and I'll come back with a plan.

Every gym is different, so after a first chat I'll send you a quote for exactly what you need, whether that's a logo refresh or a full rebrand with signage and merch.
