How I work · K.E.Y.S

8 weeks

Every brand project follows the same four stages, from our first call to final handover. I tailor the detail to your business.

  1. K

    Knowledge

    Week 1

    A 2–3 hour kick-off call to understand your brand, audience and goals, plus a look at your competitors and gathering inspiration.

    Initial planning

  2. E

    Exploration

    Weeks 2–4

    Two or three visual directions (mood boards, colour palettes, type) for you to choose from, then the core pieces, like your logo, designed in that direction.

    Discovery and research · Design direction

  3. Y

    Yield

    Weeks 4–6

    You review the concepts and I revise until it feels right. Then I build out the full set: social templates, signage, launch assets, whatever's in scope.

    Initial concepts · Final concepts

  4. S

    Succeed

    Weeks 7–8

    All your final files, guidelines on how to use them, and a short walkthrough so you're confident using the brand from day one.

    Design handover
